  \item \textbf{Efficient Model Training on Mobile Devices (Sep 2022 -- May 2025).} Designed efficient training systems for heterogeneous mobile / IoT fleets: explainable warm-start for on-device LLM personalization \citeref{pub:xpert}; gradient-corrected federated AIoT training under data and device heterogeneity \citeref{pub:feddc}; staleness-tolerant federated learning via gradient inversion \citeref{pub:aaai}.

\end{itemize}
